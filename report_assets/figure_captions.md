# Saved Figure Captions and Provenance

- **Figure 1 (`fig1_activity_distribution.png`)**: Counts of selected activity samples in the eight official protocol files; these are rows, not overlapping windows.
- **Figure 2 (`fig2_clean_confusion_matrix_rf.png`)**: Adaptive random-forest confusion matrix for the clean subject-101 held-out fold only.
- **Figure 3 (`fig3_clean_confusion_matrix_cnn.png`)**: Adaptive CNN--LSTM confusion matrix for the clean subject-101 held-out fold only.
- **Figure 4 (`fig4_faulted_confusion_matrix.png`)**: Adaptive random-forest confusion matrix for subject 108 under moderate wrist dropout; the plot does not summarize all folds.
- **Figure 5 (`fig5_macro_f1_vs_severity.png`)**: Eight-subject mean macro-F1 for three all-sensor/adaptive strategies under clean data and mild, moderate, and severe wrist dropout.
- **Figure 7 (`fig7_cost_vs_macro_f1.png`)**: Eight-subject clean mean macro-F1 versus normalized sensing-cost proxy for five random-forest strategies.
- **Figure 9 (`fig9_reliability_and_activation_timeline.png`)**: Reliability scores and activation masks from the subject-108 moderate wrist-dropout adaptive run.
- **Figure 11 (`fig11_cnn_training_curves.png`)**: CNN--LSTM training history from the final held-out fold; it does not aggregate all folds.
- **Figure 12 (`fig12_rf_feature_importance.png`)**: Wrist random-forest feature importance from the final held-out fold.
